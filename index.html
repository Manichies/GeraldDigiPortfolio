Team Leader – SSOE2 Project

Rayton Solutions Internship Project for MOE Schools, Singapore

About the project, SSOE2 project is a computer refresh project conducted at MOE schools across Singapore. We are tasked to deploy computers and laptop devices to these schools, resolve technical queries, set up and maintain IT hardware and software. Examples of software used during the project is Active Kill disk, where we utilize this software to do a sanitization and deletion of the files inside a user’s old device.

  • Led a team of 5 interns across multiple school sites to upgrade and exchange teacher's computers.
  • Managed logistics, scheduling, and equipment verification and sanitization processes.
  • Delivered on-site support for software setup, troubleshooting and handover.
  • Maintained high standards of communication, accuracy, and compliance.

In this deployment project, I took on the role of a Team Leader, which gave me valuable exposure to real-world responsibilities and workplace challenges. One of the key tasks involved managing the handover of IT devices to teachers, which required clear communication, deep understanding of procedures, and strong customer service skills. Through this experience, I learned the importance of putting the customer first, as every teacher interaction shaped the company's reputation. I also developed essential team management skills by assigning roles based on individual strengths—delegating handover duties to communicative team members and technical tasks to those with stronger IT knowledge. This approach improved efficiency, fostered team learning, and ensured a smooth handover process.
